Original licensing: https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html

The extraction keeps EasyList advertising hosts and EasyPrivacy tracking hosts
separate. It includes only plain ||host^ network rules and plain @@||host^
exceptions. It does not include cosmetic, scriptlet, option-bearing, or URL
pattern rules.

easylist.txt and easyprivacy.txt are complete upstream snapshots downloaded from
https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt and
https://easylist.to/easylist/easyprivacy.txt on 2026-10-18.
Both snapshots identify version 202610181704 and retain their upstream headers.
They are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license linked above.
Midori loads supported network and CSS rules from these snapshots before the
first online update. Unsupported syntax remains in the snapshots for attribution
and future parser upgrades; it is not executed.

compiled/*.json are generated representations of these same filter snapshots.
They retain the corresponding source attribution and license. Their source
SHA-256 hashes are embedded in each file. Regenerate with :app:generateBundledFilters.

AdGuard Scriptlets 2.5.1 (Copyright AdGuard) and uBlock Origin scriptlets at commit
c61688e27a53f9b07e07b34170a5e3e5029c35ac (Copyright Raymond Hill and contributors).
Both libraries are GPL-3.0; the original license texts are distributed alongside
the resources as LICENSE-AdGuard.txt and LICENSE-uBlock.txt.
Sources: https://github.com/AdguardTeam/Scriptlets and https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock
The catalogue and dependency wrappers are generated by scripts/vendor-scriptlets.mjs.
Exact source URLs, versions and hashes are recorded in content/scriptlets/sources.json.
